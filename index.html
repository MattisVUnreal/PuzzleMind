      How to play

      Each day there's one logic puzzle. Use the clues to work out exactly who goes with what. Every puzzle has one unique solution that pure deduction can reach. You never need to guess.

      The grid

      • Tap a cell to mark ✕ (ruled out), tap again for ● (confirmed), and again to clear it.
      • Long-press or right-click to mark ● straight away. Drag with the mouse to paint ✕ across cells.
      • Placing a ● faintly crosses out the rest of its row and column for you.
      • Fill in the Scorecard: the grid's ● marks fill it automatically, or pick from the dropdowns.

      Strokes

      You have 5 strokes to sink the puzzle. Submitting the scorecard costs a stroke. If you're wrong, you'll see how many links you got right in each category:

      🟩 all correct🟨 some correct⬛ none correct

      Stuck? A penalty stroke reveals one correct link, and it costs a stroke too.

      Scoring (par 3)

      1 stroke🏆 Hole in one
      2 strokes🐦 Birdie
      3 strokes⛳ Par
      4 strokes😬 Bogey
      5 strokes😵 Double bogey
      Out of strokes🕳️ Lost ball

      The week

      Difficulty climbs every day: Mon Gentle · Tue Steady · Wed Tricky · Thu Tough · Fri Fierce · Sat Savage · Sun Monster. From Wednesday on, you'll need "what if…?" reasoning: suppose something, follow it through, and spot the contradiction.
